**The oil situation is being dramatically underpriced.** WTI at $81 with the SPR at 40-year lows and a Hormuz deal falling apart is not a "headline risk" — it's a structural shift. Every $10 increase in oil adds roughly 0.3-0.5% to CPI. If Wednesday's CPI comes in hot because of energy, the "tame inflation" narrative collapses, and with it the rate-cut hopes that have been propping up valuations.

**Intel's $15B offering is the canary in the coal mine.** When a company beats earnings spectacularly and the stock still drops because of dilution, it tells you the market is starting to care about capital allocation again. The AI capex arms race is consuming cash at an unprecedented rate. Nvidia's $500B financing deal sounds impressive until you realize it means the AI buildout requires debt on a scale that makes the telecom bubble look quaint.

**Apple's downgrade is a warning about narrative exhaustion.** When a mega-cap stock can be downgraded to Sell on the cancellation of a product that was never confirmed to exist, it means the bull case has become so thin that any straw can break it. At 35x earnings with flat revenue growth, Apple is priced for perfection and delivering adequate.

**The Damodaran bottom line:** At these valuations, with yields at 4.70%, oil spiking, and the VIX still below 16, the market is not pricing in the risks of a potential inflation re-acceleration, a Hormuz escalation, or an AI capex bubble. The asymmetry is unfavorable. Raise cash. Wait for better prices. The market will give you a chance — it always does.
