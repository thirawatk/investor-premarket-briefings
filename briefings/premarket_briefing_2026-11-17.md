**2. Washington closed the remote-access loophole; Anthropic now wants $64B of the same dollars.**

This is the single most under-priced item in the tape: the offshore compute-rental model that made Gulf and SEA capacity economically viable just got a licensing gate. Meanwhile Anthropic prices Wednesday (825M shares at $70–78, $1.26–1.41T valuation, up to ~$64.4B raised) and trades Thursday as **ANT** on Nasdaq. A $1.3T IPO is not incremental supply — it is a competing claim on every marginal AI dollar for the next quarter.

## 🧠 The Hard Truth (Damodaran Lens)

Semiconductors are roughly **−30% from the peak while the S&P is only −6% below its high** — the index is being propped up by staples and utilities while its entire incremental earnings growth comes from the thing that is falling. That is not a market with a margin of safety; that is a market where the defensive sleeve is masking a concentrated de-rating, and where 22x forward earnings sits on a 5.12% risk-free rate that is still rising. The Fed is 95–99% priced to hike on Dec 9 into WTI −3.7% today and −13% last week, with the IEA now calling a 1.7M bpd Q4 *surplus*. You cannot simultaneously believe inflation is too hot to tolerate and that the largest input cost is collapsing — one of those two beliefs is wrong, and the market is paying full price for both.

**Nvidia is the cleanest DCF question on the board.** $156.14 is 15.7x the $2.49 Q3 consensus EPS annualised. A business compounding revenue ~90% does not trade at 15.7x unless the market has already decided the growth stops — you are being asked to underwrite either a cycle peak or a gift. The market is pricing something like 20–25% FY28 growth, not the 70% Kress guided to in August. That gap is the entire trade tonight, and it resolves in about twelve hours.

**Circle is the opposite case, and it is the one that matters for your book.** Both kill conditions you pre-committed to have now fired: CRCL closed **$57.65**, below the $60 exit, and USDC supply is **$69.3B** (Circle's own API $69.26B; DefiLlama $69.34B — two independent sources agree within 0.1%), below the $70B floor, down from ~$74B at Q3. Note what that means: the float shrank ~$4.7B in six weeks *while the Fed is hiking* — rising rates should expand reserve income and attract issuance, not shrink it. The float is going the wrong way for a reason that is not rates, and revenue is 3.6% on other people's money. This is a business-quality verdict, not a price verdict. A pre-committed rule that gets renegotiated the moment it triggers was never a rule.

**And note the tell nobody is trading on:** HYG −0.27%, LQD −0.01%, TLT flat, 10Y −3.3bp while the S&P fell 1.92%. Credit did not confirm. That is the signature of an equity-positioning and policy event, not a solvency event — which means it is tradeable, but it is *not yet* thesis-changing for the compounders.
