## 🔑 Two Big Stories

**1. Intel cracked the semis, and AMD walks into its Nov 3 print already down 11%.**
Intel's Q4 revenue guide of $15–16B vs $17.1B consensus triggered its worst single session in over 50 years (~-29%) and vaporised more than $146B of market value — the largest one-day destruction of a US chipmaker's equity on record. The damage was indiscriminate: SMH -6.98%, AMD -10.88%, AVGO -5.48%, MU -4.68%. The tell is that Intel's *data-center* revenue actually grew 78% while its PC-facing guide collapsed — so the market is now pricing a narrower AI capex story, where only the compute winners get paid and everyone levered to client/consumer silicon does not. AMD reports Tuesday after the close; it enters that print down 10.9% in a single session and needing to prove its data-center mix (58% of Q2 sales) can carry the multiple alone.

**2. The AI capex bill is now visible in cash flow — right as the discount rate goes up.**

That is the trade in one sentence: the P&L has never looked better and the cash conversion has never looked worse. Pair it with a Fed that hikes again in 32 days at 71–78% odds and a 10Y at 4.929%, and you get Friday's answer — de-rate the duration, keep the defensives.
