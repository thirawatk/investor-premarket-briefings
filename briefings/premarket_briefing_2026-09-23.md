**1. Xi-Trump Summit Kicks Off Today in Washington**

Washington sees Xi as uniquely positioned to pressure Tehran, which could de-escalate the Hormuz crisis that sent oil above $100. Any positive communique on tariffs or Iran could extend the rally; a breakdown would reverse it fast. Markets are pricing in optimism — that's the risk.

**2. Oil's 12% Crash Reshapes the Macro Picture**
WTI crude fell from $101.91 Friday to $89.67 Monday — a 12% collapse in two sessions. Trump pulled back from bombing Yemen and signaled Iran diplomacy, defusing the Hormuz premium. This is a double-edged sword: lower oil = lower inflation expectations = bullish for equities and bonds, but it also signals that the geopolitical risk premium was fragile. If Iran talks stall, oil snaps back. The Fed still has one more 25bps hike priced in (59.7% odds for Oct 28 FOMC) — lower oil helps the "skip" camp, but sticky core services inflation keeps the door open.
