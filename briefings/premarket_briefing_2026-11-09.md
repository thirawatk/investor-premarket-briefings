## 🧠 The Hard Truth (Damodaran Lens)

The market is simultaneously pricing a soft landing, one more hike and then a pause, *and* AI capex compounding at 40%+ — those three cannot all be true at a 4.86% 10-year. Every 50bp on the long end clips roughly 8–10% off the terminal value of a cash-flow stream that is 80% post-2030, which is exactly the duration the Nasdaq carries. Thursday was the discount rate and the growth rate arguing in public, and the discount rate won by $3.2tn in six hours.

Look at what you'd actually be buying in AMD: $2.09 Q3 EPS, data centre +97%, Q4 guided to $17B (+66%), 2027 revenue >80% — and yet **free cash flow collapsed to $554M, 4.1% of revenue, from 20.6% a year ago**, with inventory days at 169. At $449 on ~$16 of 2027 EPS you're paying ~28x for growth that is being funded with working capital before it becomes cash. Damodaran's question isn't "is the growth real" — it clearly is — it's "what discount rate makes this worth $449," and the answer requires long rates to stop rising. They aren't.

The clearest divergence on the tape: **HYG fell 0.31% on a day the S&P fell 4.36%.** Credit is not pricing an AI recession; equities repriced only the equity risk premium. One of them is wrong, and the tie-breaker is capex guidance from the hyperscalers, not the VIX. Meanwhile your own book is the evidence: CRCL's USDC supply has gone from **$73.9B at Sept 30 to $70.6B today** — against a **<$70B exit trigger**, that's 0.9% away — and the Q3 "beat" included **$182M of one-time ARC token presale revenue** atop reserve income that *fell* 8% Y/Y as the reserve rate dropped 61bp. Perfection is priced into the AI names and already being withdrawn from the crypto names; SGOV's 3.6% is the only line item in this portfolio being paid to wait.
