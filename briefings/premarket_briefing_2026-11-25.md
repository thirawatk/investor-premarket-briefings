My read is the discount is partly earned: a CEO who resigned over conduct, an SEC probe, an interim CEO, a permanent-CEO search, gross margins guided down, zero China data-center revenue in the Q4 guide, and now a US government that wants an equity stake. Each of those is a separate claim on the same cash flows. You are not buying a semiconductor company at 14x — you are buying a national-security instrument with a board in flux, and the terminal value is genuinely uncertain in a way DCF-tables cannot fix.

**The bond market is disagreeing with the Fed, loudly.** Dec 9 hike odds are 93–97%, three banks model three hikes, yet the 10Y has *fallen* 25 bp to 4.90%, DXY is *down* 0.45%, and gold dropped 2.5% in the same session. Momentum traders see a −2% Nasdaq; a DCF investor sees a 14x multiple on a 75% grower *and* a bond market refusing to confirm the tightening path. One of those two is badly mispriced.

So treat today as a *valuation and positioning* repricing, not a solvency event: no margin of safety has been created yet, but none has been destroyed by fundamentals either.
