- **Geopolitical risk is being systematically ignored.** Iran war premium in oil, China rare earth export bans, tariff escalation — none of this is reflected in a VIX at 14.68. The market is pricing geopolitical risk at essentially zero.

**The honest assessment:** You're being paid to take risk in a market that offers almost no margin of safety. The CPI print was "fine" — but "fine" is already priced in. The risk/reward is asymmetric to the downside. If PPI comes in hot today, the entire rate-cut narrative unwinds and the 5% AMAT pop becomes a 5% AMAT dump. Position accordingly.
