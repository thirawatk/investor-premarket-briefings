## 🧠 The Hard Truth (Damodaran Lens)

The single most unreasonable price in this market is NVDA at $162.85 — roughly **16x annualised Q3 EPS of $2.47** — for a business consensus says will grow EPS 90%. Either those earnings are a cycle peak that mean-reverts inside 24 months, or the stock is mispriced by half. The market is not scared of the AI cycle; it is applying a 5.15% discount rate to a business whose gross margin is guided *down* from 74.1% to 71.7% while revenue grows 84%. Falling incremental ROIC plus a rising cost of capital is how you get a de-rating even when the growth is real. That is precisely what you are watching.

Circle is where your margin of safety is not thin — it is absent. Excluding the $182M one-time ARC token presale, Q3 revenue *fell* to $683M from $740M a year earlier; reserve income dropped 8% as yield on reserves compressed 61bp; and USDC circulation is $73.9B, flat year-on-year. Consensus now has normalized 2026 EPS halving to $1.16 — at $62.20 that is **54x**, and even 2028's $2.11 is 29x. You are paying a growth multiple for a spread business with a flat float and rising competition. The $60 trigger is 3.5% away and you traded $61.39 after hours. The one thing that genuinely helps Circle — a Fed hiking cycle lifting reserve income — is the same thing crushing its multiple.

Now the tell of the whole cycle: Anthropic prices Wednesday and trades Thursday as ANT, raising ~$59–64B at 31x trailing revenue, with a $26B concurrent placement in which **NVIDIA puts in $10B and AMD $2.5B** — chip suppliers buying their own customer's equity at the IPO price. That is vendor financing with a valuation attached, and it lands 24 hours after NVDA reports and two days before the Fed minutes, into a 5.15% 10-year. Read the credit market instead of the tape: HYG closed **−0.05%** while semis fell 3%. Credit is not pricing an AI solvency event. It is pricing duration. Momentum traders see "AI is dead"; the bond market sees "rates are high." Both can be right, but only one of them is about these businesses.
