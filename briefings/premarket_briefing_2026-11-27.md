## 🧠 The Hard Truth (Damodaran Lens)

The market is simultaneously pricing a **certain hike** (93–95% for Dec 9) and a **bond rally** (10Y 4.848%, −22bp on the week; TLT +2.5% since Nov 18). Those two cannot both be right. Long duration only rallies into a hike if the bond market believes the hike is a mistake that will be reversed — i.e. the curve is pricing a growth break, not a tightening cycle. One of those two prices is wrong, and it is not the one with 21% of world oil behind it.

The Dow at 50,183 with 8/11 sectors green while the NDX touched −20% is not a bear market — it is a **factor unwind**. And credit confirms it: HYG +0.12% and LQD +0.48% on a day the Nasdaq was down 4.6% intraday. If this were a solvency or growth event, spreads would have widened. They did not. So stop calling it a crash and start calling it what it is: the market repricing AI capex payback periods while rotating the proceeds into everything else.

Where the margin of safety is thinnest is exactly where the book is heaviest. **AMZN at $244.43 trades 20.5x forward earnings and Simply Wall St's DCF says $483 fair value — 45% undervalued — but that model starts from TTM free cash flow of $15.1B, down from $32.6B, after $175.7B of nine-month capex.** The DCF does not say Amazon is cheap; it says Amazon is cheap *if* capex peaks in 2027 and every dollar of AI infrastructure earns its cost of capital for a decade. You are not paying 20x earnings — you are paying for a capex curve that bends exactly when management says it will. That is the definition of perfection, and the $199 stop means you are risking $45.43/share to own it.

**NVDA is the cleanest lesson in the book**: $205-ish to $149.30 in two weeks with zero change to unit demand, because the *distribution* of outcomes widened when the board's independence got questioned. A DCF captures that as a higher discount rate; a chart captures it as a broken trend; only one of them tells you what to do next. **ZS is no longer a stock** — it is 1.135 shares of CRWD, and at a 2.82% discount for a deal closing in H2 FY28 you are earning roughly 1.5%/yr of spread for taking full CRWD beta.
