## 🧠 The Hard Truth (Damodaran Lens)

The market is doing something dangerous: pricing in a rate hike while behaving as if it doesn't matter. VIX at 16.76 tells you complacency is running high — but credit markets are flashing warning signs. HYG, LQD, and TLT are all down simultaneously, meaning credit is repricing *while equities rally*. Historically, credit leads equities into trouble. If this divergence resolves the wrong way, today's FOMC could be the catalyst.

The real question isn't whether Warsh hikes — that's baked in. It's whether he signals *more* hikes ahead. A single 25bp move to 3.75–4.00% is manageable. But if the dot plot or press conference suggests a path to 4.25%+ by year-end, the entire yield curve reprices higher, and growth stocks — including your NVDA, AMD, and especially CRCL — face a valuation headwind. CRCL at $81.76 is now 25% underwater from your $109 entry; a hawkish Fed makes the stablecoin/growth narrative even harder to sustain.
