Combined with PCE inflation still at 3.7% YoY (down from 4.1% in May but well above 2% target), this is the stagflation narrative markets have feared. The consumer remains the last pillar holding up — Employment Cost Index today will test that.

## 5. The Hard Truth — Damodaran Lens

Let's be brutally honest about what we're seeing.

**The AI capex emperor has no clothes — or rather, it has some clothes, but they're very expensive.** Microsoft proved AI spending can generate returns (Azure +43%). Meta proved the opposite — spending $42B in a quarter while free cash flow evaporates by 91% is not "investing in the future," it's lighting money on fire and hoping the smoke spells "AGI." The market's message is clear: show me the revenue, or I'll show you the door.

**The 30-year Treasury at 5.23% is the real story everyone is ignoring.** This is the highest since 2007. The long end of the curve is telling you that inflation is not transitory — it's structural. The Fed's 9-3 dissent confirms the hawks are gaining ground. If the Employment Cost Index comes in hot today, September hike odds will blow past 50%. The housing market, already under pressure with 30-year mortgages above 6.8%, will crack further.

**Valuation reality check:** The S&P 500 at 7,423 is trading at ~22x forward earnings. That's not crazy expensive if earnings grow 15%+ as consensus expects. But if GDP is decelerating to 1.5% with 3.7% inflation, that earnings growth assumption is on thin ice. The Q2 GDP miss is a warning shot.

**On Iran:** This is no longer a "geopolitical noise" event. Saudi Arabia's direct involvement, the Egypt drone strike, and the widening theater mean this could easily become a sustained oil supply disruption. The $90 Brent crude level is not a spike — it could be the new floor. If this escalates further, add 50-100 bps to inflation expectations and kiss the September rate cut goodbye forever.

**The honest bottom line:** You're in a market that's pricing in AI-driven productivity growth while the real economy is slowing and inflation is sticky. That's a bet, not a certainty. Position accordingly — quality over speculation, cash as a position, and don't chase the Microsoft bounce.
