## 5. The Hard Truth (Damodaran Lens)

**You are paying record multiples for cash flows discounted at 2007-era long rates.** The S&P trades near 7,700 while its discount rate — the 10Y at 4.74%, 30Y at post-2007 highs — has risen ~50 bp in weeks without equities blinking. That gap resolves one of two ways: either earnings growth accelerates enough to justify both numbers simultaneously, or one market is wrong. Bond markets correct faster than equity markets when they're the ones wrong.

On the AI complex specifically: NVDA at −9% into the print means *some* perfection has already been repriced — but the question a DCF investor asks is different from what momentum asks. Even if NVDA beats, the marginal buyer must believe hyperscaler capex grows into perpetuity at margins that survive competition (AMD, custom silicon). At these weights, an in-line quarter with cautious guidance does more damage than a beat helps.
