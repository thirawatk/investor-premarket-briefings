## 5. The Hard Truth — Damodaran Lens

Let's be blunt: at a CAPE of 41, you are paying an extraordinary premium for earnings that are being inflated by a handful of mega-cap tech companies whose AI-driven revenue growth may not be as durable as the market assumes. The 31% S&P 500 EPS growth is impressive, but it is concentrated — strip out the Magnificent Seven and the picture looks far less heroic.

The Fed is in a bind. Three dissented in favor of a hike. Inflation has cooled, but the 10Y yield at 4.70% and a steeper curve suggest the bond market is pricing in either (a) stronger growth or (b) more inflation risk than the Fed acknowledges. Either way, the "Fed put" is further out of the money than the VIX at 14.25 implies.

Oil at $78-84 with an active geopolitical chokepoint is not "priced in" — it's tolerated. One escalation in the Strait of Hormuz and Brent is back at $100+. The market's complacency is priced in the VIX, not in the actual risk distribution.

Copper at all-time highs is genuinely bullish for the AI capex cycle, but it's also a commodity supercycle signal that historically ends with a rotation out of growth and into value. The market hasn't had that reckoning yet.

**Bottom line:** This is a market that is expensive, concentrated, and underpricing tail risk. Momentum is real, but so is mean reversion. Own quality, demand margin of safety, and don't confuse a bull market with genius.
